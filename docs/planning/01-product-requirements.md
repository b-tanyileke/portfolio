## 1. Vision

Build an engineering website that serves as the central hub for my professional journey. The website will communicate what I have built, problems I've solved, and how I continue growing as an engineer.

Rather than functioning as a traditional portfolio, the website will present a collection of engineering case studies, technical writing, open-source contributions, and career milestones that evolve throughout my career.

The website should remain relevant for many years, growing alongside my technical skills and professional experience.

---

## 2. Objectives

The website should:

- Establish a professional online presence.
- Showcase high-quality AI and software engineering projects.
- Demonstrate engineering thinking through detailed project case studies.
- Highlight practical experience with machine learning, computer vision, LLMs, and software development.
- Serve as a single location that recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and technical peers can reference.
- Provide a platform for documenting continuous learning through engineering notes and technical articles.
- Integrate naturally with GitHub and future open-source contributions.

---

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary

- Recruiters
- Hiring Managers
- Technical Interviewers

### Secondary

- Software Engineers

---

## 4. Product Vision

The website should leave visitors with the impression that its owner builds practical AI-powered software systems and communicates engineering decisions clearly.

The website should emphasize engineering depth over project quantity.

Every major project should explain:

- The problem
- The motivation
- The technical approach
- Architectural decisions
- Challenges encountered
- Lessons learned
- Future improvements

---

## 5. Brand Identity

The website represents:

> Practical AI Engineering for Real-World Problems

Core characteristics:

- Engineering-first
- Practical
- Curious
- Thoughtful
- Transparent
- Continuously improving

---

## 6. Design Principles

The product should follow these principles.

### Authenticity

Every statement must be truthful and defensible during interviews.

### Depth over Quantity

Three exceptional projects are more valuable than fifteen incomplete ones.

### Explain Engineering

Focus on decisions, trade-offs, and lessons—not just technologies.

### Continuous Growth

The website should evolve naturally throughout my career.

### Simplicity

Content should be easy to navigate and free from unnecessary visual clutter.

---

## 7. Functional Requirements

The initial release should include:

- Home
- About
- Projects
- Resume
- Contact

Each project should have its own dedicated page.

The website should support:

- Responsive design
- GitHub integration
- Resume download
- External project links
- Video demonstrations
- Screenshots
- Architecture diagrams

## 9. Success Criteria

A visitor should understand within one minute:

- Who I am.
- What I build.
- Which technologies I use.
- How I solve engineering problems.
- Why they should continue exploring the website.

Recruiters should leave with confidence that I can contribute to real-world engineering projects.

---

## 10. Long-Term Goal

This website is intended to become the permanent home of my professional identity.

As my career develops, the website will grow from a project showcase into a complete engineering website documenting projects, technical writing, open-source contributions, research, and professional achievements.

The portfolio is not the final product.

The website itself is an ongoing engineering project.
