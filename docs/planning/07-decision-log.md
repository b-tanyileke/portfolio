## Purpose

This document records significant product, design, and engineering decisions made throughout the development of the engineering website.

The goal is to preserve the reasoning behind major decisions so that future changes remain consistent with the product vision.

This document is intended to evolve throughout the lifetime of the project.

---

## Decision Format

Each decision should include:

- Decision number
- Decision
- Date
- Context
- Rationale
- Consequences
- Status

---

## Decisions

### DEC-001

#### Decision (DEC-001)

Position the project as an **Engineering Website** rather than a traditional portfolio.

#### Context (DEC-001)

The long-term vision extends beyond showcasing projects to documenting engineering thinking, learning, and professional growth.

#### Rationale (DEC-001)

The term "engineering website" better reflects the broader purpose of the project and supports future expansion.

#### Consequences (DEC-001)

The website architecture prioritizes case studies, engineering notes, and long-term evolution over static portfolio pages.

#### Status (DEC-001)

Accepted

---

### DEC-002

#### Decision (DEC-002)

Adopt a documentation-first development process.

#### Context (DEC-002)

The project aims to demonstrate engineering methodology as well as implementation.

#### Rationale (DEC-002)

Planning before implementation improves consistency and creates reusable documentation.

#### Consequences (DEC-002)

Major planning documents are completed before frontend development begins.

#### Status (DEC-002)

Accepted

---

### DEC-003

#### Decision (DEC-003)

Separate Design Principles from the Design System.

#### Context (DEC-003)

The project distinguishes philosophy from implementation standards.

#### Rationale (DEC-003)

Principles explain *why* decisions exist.

The Design System explains *how* they are applied.

#### Consequences (DEC-003)

Design discussions remain easier to maintain as the system evolves.

#### Status (DEC-003)

Accepted

---

### DEC-004

#### Decision (DEC-004)

Rename Content Strategy to Content Architecture.

#### Context (DEC-004)

The project focuses on organizing and connecting information rather than editorial planning.

#### Rationale (DEC-004)

"Architecture" more accurately describes the role of the document.

#### Consequences (DEC-004)

Future documents will reference Content Architecture as the source of truth for information design.

#### Status (DEC-004)

Accepted

---

### DEC-005

#### Decision (DEC-005)

Every project begins with a Project Narrative.

#### Context (DEC-005)

Many technical portfolios begin immediately with implementation details.

#### Rationale (DEC-005)

Readers should first understand the problem being solved before learning how it was solved.

#### Consequences (DEC-005)

Every project follows a consistent storytelling structure.

#### Status (DEC-005)

Accepted

---

### DEC-006

#### Decision (DEC-006)

Adopt the principle of "Evidence Over Claims."

#### Context (DEC-006)

Engineering ability should be demonstrated rather than asserted.

#### Rationale (DEC-006)

Projects, documentation, repositories, diagrams, and metrics provide stronger evidence than self-descriptions.

#### Consequences (DEC-006)

Claims throughout the website should be supported whenever possible.

#### Status (DEC-006)

Accepted

---

### DEC-007

#### Decision (DEC-007)

Prioritize typography and layout before color selection.

#### Context (DEC-007)

Readability is the primary goal of the website.

#### Rationale (DEC-007)

Typography and spacing contribute more to perceived quality than decorative styling.

#### Consequences (DEC-007)

The design process emphasizes structure before visual styling.

#### Status (DEC-007)

Accepted
